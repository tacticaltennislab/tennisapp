## Needs a human to confirm

- **Incidental third-party marks.** The racquet and a court umbrella in the frame carry small, out-of-focus brand marks. They are not featured and no brand is named, but a lawyer should confirm this is acceptable for commercial marketing use.
- **Model release.** Pexels says nothing about whether the player signed a model release. The License prohibits showing identifiable people in a bad light and implying endorsement, and the site does neither, but Pexels does not guarantee every third-party right is cleared.
- **Resolution.** The page advertises 1920x1080, but the file the CDN serves is 1280x720. It is enough at the size shown, and a higher-resolution original would sharpen it on large retina screens.
